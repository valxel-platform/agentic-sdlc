# Dental constitution

Starter draft. Regulatory articles need compliance counsel review before they are treated as complete.

- **DNT-1** Patient treatment and health records are PHI and get the same controls as CLN-1. (Policy, Test)
- **DNT-2** Consent is captured, versioned and checked before treatment and before any marketing use. (Test)
- **DNT-3** X-rays and intraoral photos are PHI; marketing use needs separate explicit consent. (Policy, Test)
- **DNT-4** Booking never double-books a provider, room or device. (Test)
- **DNT-5** Memberships, packages and payments run through PAY. (Policy)
- **DNT-6** Messaging honors opt-outs and channel rules (for example TCPA and CAN-SPAM in the US). (Test)
